UML Studio

Draw a diagram, press Check, and get it marked — not just right or wrong, but why. Your work is saved in this browser, so you can come back to it.

Place a node

Click a tool under Nodes, then click the canvas. Type its name, attributes and methods in the inspector on the right. Drag nodes to move them.

Draw a relationship

Click a tool under Relationships, then click the two nodes in order. Direction matters: child → parent for inheritance, owner → part for the diamonds.

Get it marked

Press Check my diagram. Mistakes are listed first with the reasoning. Delete removes the selected item; Esc cancels a tool.
